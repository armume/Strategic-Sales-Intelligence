# Retail-Sales-Analysis

## Objective
Analyze sales performance to identify declines, outliers, customer behavior, 
and provide data-driven business recommendations.

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset (2015–2018)

## Business Questions
1. What factors explain the decline in sales?
2. Which categories underperform?
3. Which customer segments are buying less?
4. Are there relevant temporal trends?
5. Which regions show the greatest impact?
6. Where are the sales outliers and why?
7. What strategic recommendations can be made?

## Key Insights
- Technology leads sales but depends heavily on outliers
- Consumer dominates revenue, increasing concentration risk
- Corporate segment shows growth potential
- Strong seasonality in Q3–Q4

## Business Recommendations
- Strengthen Corporate segment with targeted offers
- Reduce dependency on Consumer revenue
- Leverage Q4 seasonality with inventory planning
- Treat high-value outliers as strategic clients, not anomalies
